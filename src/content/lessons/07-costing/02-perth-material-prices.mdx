Every material on your take-off exists at three or four different prices in Perth,
depending on which door you walk through. The spread is not ten per cent. On soil
and mulch it is three to four times. On plants it is routinely double. Nobody
advertises this, because the cheap end of the market is not trying to sell to you.

This lesson is about where the trade buys, what an account gets you, and the two
numbers that decide whether you saved anything: the bagged-versus-bulk break-even,
and the delivery charge.

One warning before the numbers. Every price below was read off a published Perth
supplier page on **2 August 2026**. They show you the shape of the market; they do
not go into your quote. Prices move constantly — Soils Ain't Soils added a [10 per
cent fuel levy to every product on 24 March
2026](https://soilsaintsoils.com.au/product/black-mulch/), the kind of change that
never reaches the headline price. Ring, get today's number, write the date beside it.

## What a professional does at this stage

A landscaper does not price materials job by job. They hold accounts, and an account
does four things you cannot replicate: a standing discount off list, priority when
stock is short, thirty-day terms, and someone who tells them what actually landed
this week.

What you can replicate is the discipline. Price the same material at three tiers
before ordering, buy the bulk lines in bulk and the fiddly lines retail, and never
let a delivery fee be a surprise.

| Tier | Who it is | Sells to | You pay | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Retail garden centre** | Dawsons, Zanthorrea, Waldecks, Guildford Town | Anyone | Highest, per plant or per bag | Advice, single specimen plants, small quantities, weekends |
| **Landscape supply yard** | Soils Ain't Soils, Green Life Soil, Perth Sand and Soil, Supa Soils | Anyone, better rates on account | Mid | Soil, mulch, sand, crushed limestone, gravel, by the cubic metre |
| **Manufacturer or quarry direct** | Midland Brick, block and stone yards | Anyone, trade pricing on account | Mid to low, plus cartage | Pavers, limestone blocks, aggregates, anything by the pallet |
| **Wholesale nursery** | Benara, Domus, Wanneroo Plant Farm | ABN holders only | Lowest per plant | Nothing, unless you have a business — but it sets the floor you are being marked up from |

Benara [state it plainly](https://www.benaranurseries.com/our-trademarts/): trade and
wholesale only, no public sales, account requires a registered business with a valid
ABN. That is the tier your garden centre buys from. Knowing it exists tells you the
markup is real — not that you can get around it.

## Bulk versus bagged: the break-even is not close

Do this arithmetic once and you will never forget it. A cubic metre is 1,000 litres.

Green Life Soil sell the same soil builder [bagged at $16.50 for 25
litres](https://greenlifesoil.com.au/bags) and [in bulk at $168 per cubic
metre](https://greenlifesoil.com.au/bulk-garden-supplies-perth/). Forty bags make a
cubic metre, so bagged costs **$660 per cubic metre** — nearly four times the bulk
price for the identical product. Their five-bag pack rate still works out at $600.

So the rule is blunt: **anything over about half a cubic metre goes bulk.** Half a
cubic metre is twenty bags. If you have twenty bags on a trolley, you are in the
wrong shop.

The other half of the rule is that a small bulk load is not penalised. Soils Ain't
Soils price [black mulch at $113.50 per cubic metre and $79.50 per 6x4
trailer](https://soilsaintsoils.com.au/product/black-mulch/), and a
6x4 trailer holds 0.7 cubic metres — $113.57 per cubic metre, exactly pro rata. Their
two-hour trailer hire is free with a valid WA licence. If you have a tow bar, small
loads cost nothing extra per unit; scale only spreads the delivery fee.

Bagged still wins for potting mix, anything you need half a bag of, and anything that
has to go through a house.

## Delivery, minimums and access

Delivery is separate and it is not small. Green Life Soil quote roughly [$50 to $120
for a special delivery](https://greenlifesoil.com.au/delivery) within about 20 km of
Middle Swan, six cubic metres per load. Eco Landscape Supply impose a [two cubic
metre minimum and offer no pickup at
all](https://ecolandscapesupply.com.au/delivery/), with up to five days lead time.
Turf delivery to the metro area runs [$150 to $220 on top of the
turf](https://www.wcturf.com.au/turf-ordering/).

Three things then decide whether the truck can do its job:

1. **Volume per load.** A tipper carries up to 14 tonnes, which [Supa Soils translate
   as](https://www.supasoils.com.au/pages/delivery-information) about 10 cubic metres
   of sands and stones but 12 of landscape mix or mulch. Weight fills the truck, not
   volume.
2. **Vehicle type.** Limestone pallets and bulka bags need a truck-mounted crane, a
   different and dearer vehicle. Book it when you order, not on the day.
3. **Where it lands.** Delivery is to the kerb alignment. If the truck enters your
   property you carry the risk for the driveway and anything under it. Drivers refuse
   soft sand, and a car parked across the drop point earns you a second delivery fee.

Order all of one product at once. Soil, mulch and crushed limestone vary batch to
batch, and two deliveries a fortnight apart will not match.

## Hardscape: the numbers that anchor everything else

Midland Brick is the WA manufacturer and one of the few that [publishes per square
metre prices](https://www.midlandbrick.com.au/category/pavers). Checked 2 August
2026: Easilock 80 Granite $73.66/m², Coachlock 80 Stonewash $89.97/m², Flagstone 80
Granite $100.33/m² — inclusive of GST, excluding cartage, some pallets carrying a
refundable deposit. Their [reconstituted limestone
blocks](https://www.midlandbrick.com.au/category/blocks/limestone-blocks) price by
the pallet: half blocks $408.28, split blocks $403.52, metre blocks $721.86, key
blocks $918.15.

Underneath all of it sits crushed limestone, [$80 per cubic metre plus
delivery](https://www.perthsandandsoil.com.au/product/19mm-crushed-limestone/) at
Perth Sand and Soil. Buy it by the cubic metre at one yard and by the tonne at
another and you are comparing nothing. Ask which unit first.

Before committing to a block wall, read the [weight
table](https://www.meteorstone.com.au/limestone-blocks-perth/): reconstituted
limestone runs from 24 kg for a 500 mm mini brick to 225 kg for a 1000 mm flat
block. That
number, not the price, decides whether the wall is hand-laid or needs a machine.

**Safety, plainly:** cutting pavers, limestone or concrete produces respirable
crystalline silica, and WorkSafe WA names paver cutting and laying specifically. Cut
wet, use on-tool extraction, vacuum rather than sweep, never use compressed air, and
wear proper respiratory protection. Engineered stone slabs, panels and benchtops
are [banned in WA](https://www.worksafe.wa.gov.au/silica). This is a
disease you get years later from a weekend you barely remember.

## Plants, turf and retic

The same shrub sold as tubestock, in a 140 mm pot at a garden centre and in a 200 mm
pot in flower can differ by a factor of ten. Same plant, eighteen months apart. None
of the three tiers below publish a full price list, so ring for today's numbers.

- **Tubestock** from a community nursery like [APACE in North
  Fremantle](https://www.apacewa.org.au/what-we-do/retail-and-wholesale-nursery/) —
  local Swan Coastal Plain provenance, retail up to 200 plants over the counter. Best
  value here for mass planting; needs a careful first summer.
- **Council schemes.** Many Perth councils subsidise natives for residents — the City
  of Fremantle has run [$1.50 tubes through
  APACE](https://mysay.fremantle.wa.gov.au/greening-fremantle/news_feed/green-your-garden-with-subsidised-plants)
  each May. Check yours before you buy a single native.
- **Retail garden centres**, filtered through the [Waterwise Garden Centre
  list](https://greenlifeindustrywa.com.au/waterwise-garden-centres/) — retail price,
  real advice, a fair trade for one specimen tree.

West Coast Turf publish $11/m² for Winter Green couch and Zulu kikuyu up to $15/m²
for Santa Ana and soft leaf buffalo, and [say
openly](https://www.wcturf.com.au/turf-ordering/) that the same variety trades
between $8.80 and $15.50 across the market. Turf is alive: cut one day, dying the
next. Book it to a laying date, not a delivery date.

Retic trade counters such as [WA Reticulation
Supplies](https://wareticulationsupplies.com.au) at Armadale, Midland and Rockingham
sell to trade and public alike and carry the full Hunter and Toro ranges rather than
the blister-pack subset. The old Total Eden branches now trade as Nutrien Water, so
search the current name. None publish prices online — price the same parts list at
two counters.

## Available now versus on order

Ask two questions of every supplier: *is it in stock today*, and *what is the lead
time if not*. Midland Brick have had clay products on backorder. Turf varieties come
and go. Plant availability is seasonal, which is why the Water Corporation
[Waterwise Plants
directory](https://www.watercorporation.com.au/Waterwise/Waterwise-plants) lets you
filter by it — use that filter before a species reaches your
[planting plan](/curriculum/documentation/planting-plan-and-schedule/).

## Getting it home legally

Sand, soil or debris coming off an unsecured or uncovered load is littering under the
Litter Act 1979 and carries a [$200 on-the-spot
fine](https://www.wa.gov.au/service/environment/unsecured-load-unsafe-road). Cover
the load, and do not fill a 6x4 trailer with wet soil unless you know its rating —
0.7 cubic metres of wet sand is well over a tonne.
