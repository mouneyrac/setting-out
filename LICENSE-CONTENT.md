## Disclaimer

This is educational material, not professional advice. Regulatory detail in
Western Australia — sprinkler rosters, permit thresholds, council fees, rebate
programs — changes. Every regulatory claim carries a "checked on" date and a
link to the primary source. **Always confirm with your local council, the Water
Corporation, DWER or a licensed professional before you build, dig or spend.**

Structural work, electrical work, plumbing, pool fencing and gas work in Western
Australia are regulated. Where a licensed trade is legally required, this course
says so — and you should use one.
